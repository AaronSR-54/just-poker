# Release notes

## 1.2.1 (versionCode 6)

<en-US>
Online multiplayer is here.

• Play private online tables with 2 to 4 friends — no accounts needed.
• Create a room and invite friends with a 4-character code or a QR.
• Reconnect and resume your table if the connection drops.
• Single-player against 9 AI rivals, in three difficulties.
• Built-in guide to all 10 hand rankings.
• Spanish and English. Single-player is fully offline: no ads, no accounts, no real money.
</en-US>
