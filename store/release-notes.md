# Release notes

## 1.4.0 (versionCode 10)

<en-US>
Invites now open the app.

• Scan or tap an invite link on Android and it opens the app directly, straight to the table.
• Still no accounts: invite friends with a 4-character code or a QR.
• Single-player against 9 AI rivals, in three difficulties — fully offline.
• Built-in guide to all 10 hand rankings.
• Spanish and English. No ads and no real money.
